## Draft: small living rooms

Intent: help a customer plan visible curtain area, furniture clearance and stacking space without promising that one colour/style makes every room look larger. Outline: inspect the room's constraints; compare open and closed curtain positions; consider relation to walls and furniture; check actual hardware; compare physical samples; list questions for an enquiry. Add original diagrams/room photos only when approved and licensed. Link to the living-room guide, `/measure` and Studio. Do not imply fitted services or specific manufactured widths.

## Draft: common selection mistakes

Intent: a practical checklist before deciding. Outline: relying on a screen colour; confusing a fibre name with a performance rating; measuring only the glass; forgetting heading and stacking assumptions; estimating without pattern repeats; assuming generic cleaning advice; overlooking evening privacy; treating a visualization as a guarantee. Use specific neutral examples, not fabricated customer anecdotes. Link to existing comparison and measurement guides. Publish only after a useful original draft and business review.

## Editorial workflow and quality

Use real paragraph sections, one H1, descriptive H2 headings and contextual links. New guides use `data/site/guides.json`; original content remains in `data/site/editorial.json`. Required fields: stable slug/title/description/image/body; sections contain heading/paragraphs. Optional: real imageAlt, publication/update dates and relatedSlugs. Author/editor fields must not be invented. Publisher identity is the real brand, not a fictional staff author.

Before publication, check accuracy against documented supplier specifications for any product-specific assertion. Generic textile observations are not a substitute for an actual composition, tested blackout rating or care label. Ask for physical samples; keep Studio limitations visible. Do not write unverified washing, UV resistance, fire performance, insulation, manufacturing, shipping or installation promises.
